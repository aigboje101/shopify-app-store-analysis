# shopify-app-store-analysis
Triple Ten Sprint 5 Project

Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights
Dashboard: Shopify App Store Analysis

Reporting Period: Latest available data

Key Insight

The App Category with the highest average reviews is 'Productivity' at 4.39. This is only the highest by 0.04 from the second which is 'Sales and Conversion.' The Ratings of all Categories  average 4.19, with the lowest ones being least 4.00. This is especially the case in recent years. Ratings would mostly vary in the years before 2020. Free apps also tend to have slightly higher ratings at 4.20 with the ones with a plan average 4.19, a minimal difference. Developer Engagement does not seem to matter much either as Reviews with Replies also share a 0.01 difference (4.20 with - 4.19 without).

Business Impact

Reviews should be on the higher end no matter what category, people seem to have grown more generous in recent years review-wise. Attatching a Plan to the app only seems to make a minor difference, which is good for making money. The 'Sales and Conversion' Category seems to be the most consistently high placing but the 'Productivity' category is more likely to land a higher rating according to trends. 

Recommendation

Examining patterns in negative reviews is a good way to see directly what someone may take issue with. Highest and Lowest Rated Categories should also be monitored for changes in growth. This will constantly tell us which are thriving.
