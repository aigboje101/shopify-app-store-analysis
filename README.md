# shopify-app-store-analysis
Triple Ten Sprint 5 Project

Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights
Dashboard: Shopify App Store Analysis

Reporting Period: Latest available data

Key Insight

The App Category with the highest average reviews is 'Productivity' at 4.39. This is only the highest by 0.04 from the second which is 'Sales and Conversion.' The Ratings of all Categories  average 4.19, with the lowest ones being least 4.00. This is especially the case in recent years. Ratings would mostly vary in the years before 2020. Free apps also tend to have lower ratings, probably due to greater accesibility. Reviews where the Developers reply also tend to have slightly higher ratings.

Business Impact

Reviews should be on the higher end no matter what category, people seem to have grown more generous in recent years review-wise. Apps with a price attatched seem to get slightly better reviews though less reviews in general, good for money but bad for exposure. The 'Sales and Conversion' Category seems to be the most consistently high placing but the 'Productivity' category is more likely to land a higher rating according to trends.

Recommendation

Customer reviews seem to be mostly generous. Engaging with the reviews as a developer also makes it more likely to get a good review. The category of an app itself doesn't seem to greatly affect the review itself though, this shouldn't be an area of concern. Whether an app offers a free plan or not also affects the review, but by a marginal amount. Any external interaction you could have with a reviewer to raise the rating of an app may only provide a slight increase.
